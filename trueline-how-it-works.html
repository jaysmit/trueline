What each side controls

The borrower supplies reasons. You supply the decision.

Steps 1–2 · setup

One link, no accounts

You enter the borrower and send. They upload on their phone. Statements are read once and removed, and only the transaction list is kept.

Steps 3–4 · borrower

AI does the finding, they do the explaining

The transactions that may not be ongoing are surfaced with a reason. The borrower sets what each should be and says why, and can open any category to raise one AI missed.

Step 5 · broker

Nothing moves until you act

Requests arrive as proposals. You set the final amount, write your own note, and approve, reduce or decline. Refusals are recorded exactly like approvals.

Step 6 · the file

The assessment writes itself

Documented against assessed, every adjustment with both notes and any evidence attached, a category breakdown, and an audit entry for each action.

See it with your own file.

We’ll run a demo assessment against a real statement set and show you what needs a reason and what the file note looks like at the end.
